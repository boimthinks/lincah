# TEMPLAT NASKAH UGC — Format Storyboard Studio (Google Flow)

Templat ini untuk naskah UGC super singkat (12–18 detik, 2–3 klip @ 6 detik) yang masuk ke kolom `fullMarkdown` di Google Flow.

---

## Format Standar

```markdown
# [Judul Video UGC]

> Gaya Visual: First-person POV hand-held phone camera shot, candid organic style, natural lighting, right-hand-drive Indonesian MPV.

<!-- scene-id: -->
### POV HP - KLIP 1: HOOK (0:00–0:06)

> [Arah kamera POV hand-held, aksi 6 detik pertama]

[Deskripsi adegan cuplikan hook]

**TEXT OVERLAY:** "[Teks hook 3-5 kata di layar]"

##### CUT TO:

<!-- scene-id: -->
### POV HP - KLIP 2: BUKTI (0:06–0:12)

> [Arah kamera POV, tunjukkan kenyamanan/layanan]

[Deskripsi adegan cuplikan bukti/benefit]

**TEXT OVERLAY:** "[Teks bukti/layanan di layar]"

##### CUT TO:

<!-- scene-id: -->
### POV HP - KLIP 3: CTA (0:12–0:18)

> [Arah kamera POV, momen closing/senyum/rekomendasi]

[Deskripsi adegan penutup]

**TEXT OVERLAY:** "[Teks CTA + sebut Lincah Travel]"

##### FADE OUT.
```
